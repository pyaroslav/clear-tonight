Does the correction actually help?

How many nights of history does it need?

Brier score (lower is better), averaged over 6 cities × 6 months, training only on the last K nights before each month. TabPFN v2 is the only 15-feature model that beats the raw forecast from the first week; the 15-feature logistic regression and gradient boosting need two to four months before they're even as good as the raw forecast.

Updated . Probabilities come from the GFS forecast issued about 24 hours ahead, corrected per city by TabPFN v2. Truth for training is ERA5 reanalysis cloud cover (a model-assisted reconstruction, not a human looking up).
